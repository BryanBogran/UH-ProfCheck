# Supported campuses

ProfCheck supports these enrollment portals. To add your school, read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and open a campus request.

**Status**
- **Testing:** being built and tested. Not in the store release.
- **Beta:** in the store release, off by default. Turn it on in ProfCheck settings.
- **Supported:** in the store release and verified on every release.
- **Paused:** off by default because a portal change hasn't been fixed or verified within 14 days. You can still turn it on in settings.
